# 05: Primitives A — form controls

**What to build:** Button, Checkbox, Switch, SearchInput, and TimePicker share the new visual language across every state and both themes, with their public prop APIs unchanged.

**Blocked by:** 03 (Token layer).

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] All five primitives are styled exclusively through semantic tokens; no raw values.
- [ ] Hover, active, disabled, invalid where applicable, and focus-visible states are defined and meet WCAG AA in both themes.
- [ ] Prop APIs are unchanged; any new variants are additive.
- [ ] The primitives are verified on the screens that use them most in both themes and at both breakpoints.
